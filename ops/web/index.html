How the engine works

A fully-automated, long-only paper engine for the AI-infrastructure equity complex. It reads industry writing, forms a machine thesis, sizes names mechanically, and trades on a schedule — no human in the loop. Everything here is paper money and none of it is advice.

STAGE 01

Ingest

Pulls supply- and demand-side writing (SemiAnalysis, Stratechery, Latent Space…), HuggingFace Daily Papers, and SEC 8-K filings into a local store.

STAGE 02

Thesis

An LLM (opencode, qwen3.7-max) reads the corpus — searching a local vector index on demand — and writes a layer tilt, a market-regime call, and the book.

STAGE 03

Momentum

Within each layer, names are ranked by 12–1 momentum (trailing-year return, ex the last month). The strongest rise to the top.

STAGE 04

Assemble

Each layer's budget is split by momentum, capped per name. Thin conviction stays in cash — the book can sit below fully invested.

STAGE 05

Trade

Once a weekday the engine rebalances the Alpaca paper account, but only when its own urgency gate says so — an unchanged thesis trades nothing.

Why five layers

The AI build-out is a stack, physical foundation to value-capture: power feeds fabrication, which yields compute, which fills infrastructure, which runs the platform. Baseline weights lean toward the layers the index underweights and away from platform's concentrated core. The universe is a curated ticker→layer map of ~58 names; anything unmapped can't be held.

The grey era — v1

Before Layercake, a v1 scorer (grey on the chart) ran this same $100k account from inception — Mar 24, 2026 — holding mega-cap AI names and rebalancing on drift, until the Layercake migration on Jun 29. Layercake replaced it with a disciplined, cash-aware, layer-budgeted book.
